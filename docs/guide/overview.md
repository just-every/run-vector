# Overview

Vector is an open-source issue tracker for one team or one business. Every report that comes in gets a clear decision, and everyone sees what is happening now, what is next, and why. You run it yourself, it is free, and the source is MIT licensed.

## How it fits together

- **Current** is the team's shared list. Each open issue sits in one section: **Needs decision**, **Now**, **Next** or **Recently done**.
- **Needs decision** holds what has come in and not been decided yet. Someone makes the call: accept it, defer it to a set time, or decline it with a reason. See [Decide what comes in](triage.md).
- **My work** is your open work: everything assigned to you that is not finished yet.
- **Issues** and the **Board** let you browse, filter, sort and group all the work, and save the views you use.
- **Projects** and **Cycles** group work toward a goal and a stretch of time. Unfinished work rolls into the next cycle when one ends.
- **Inbox** collects your assignments, mentions, comments on issues you follow, and deferred reports coming back.
- **Settings** is where you invite people, set the workspace time, and look after teams, workflows, labels, projects and cycles.

Every change to an issue is kept in its history: who changed what, from what, and when.

## Where to start

1. [Run it on your computer](install.md) and sign in as the owner.
2. [Decide what comes in](triage.md) from Needs decision.
3. [Plan and track the work](work.md) in Issues, the Board, Projects and Cycles.
4. [Invite your team](team.md) when you are ready.
