# Your team and settings

## Invite people

Open **Settings** and invite people by email. Everyone you invite can do the same things: Vector has no separate roles. They sign in with a single-use link sent to their email address.

Removing someone signs them out everywhere, cancels their unused links and invitations, and keeps their past work in the history. Vector always keeps at least one person who can sign in.

## Workspace time

Settings sets the workspace timezone. Cycles and due dates use it.

## Teams and workflows

Each team has its own identifier prefix (like `FLW`) and its own workflow. A new team starts with Intake, Backlog, Unstarted, Started, Completed, Canceled and Duplicate. You can add, rename and reorder statuses; removing one asks which status its issues should move to. Archiving a team keeps all its issues and history and stops new work going to it.

## Labels

Labels can be renamed, recoloured and deleted. Deleting a label removes it from issues and from saved views.

## Notifications

Each person chooses which notifications reach their Inbox and whether assignments and mentions also arrive by email. See [Notifications and email](notifications.md).
